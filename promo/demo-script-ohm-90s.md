# ohm — the 90-second demo

*For a booth, a brokerage sales meeting, or a hallway. One phone, one iPad (optional), one printed table tent.*

**Setup (before anyone's watching):** a demo open house already created for a recognizable local address; kiosk open on the iPad; your phone on the event's live view; the table tent printed and standing.

---

**0:00 — The hook** *(hold up a paper sign-in sheet)*

> "This is where open house evidence goes to die. Nobody can read the third name, half the emails are fake, and the seller never sees any of it. Ninety seconds — watch what Saturday looks like instead."

**0:10 — Sign in as a visitor** *(hand them the iPad, or point at the table tent's QR)*

> "You're the visitor. Tap anywhere. Name, phone, email — that's the whole form."

*While they type:* "Notice what it's **not** asking — no budget interrogation, no 'how did you hear about us.' Thirty seconds, six taps. And the globe up there runs the whole thing in Spanish, Chinese, Korean, or Vietnamese."

**0:30 — The packet lands** *(their phone buzzes)*

> "Check your phone. The home's photos, the description, the 3D tour, my card — delivered before you've left the entryway. That's the trade: thirty honest seconds, and the house is in your pocket."

**0:45 — The agent's phone** *(show the live view)*

> "Meanwhile, on my phone: the count just went up. There you are — name, time, and I can see you came through the kiosk, not the QR code. This list is mine — export it as a spreadsheet tonight."

**1:00 — The report** *(open a finished report from a past demo event)*

> "Sunday night, this goes to the seller. Visitors measured. Packets delivered, opened. This weekend against last weekend. What I did after — every follow-up. And the part sellers remember: **no visitor names ever** — their neighbors signed in, but the seller sees honest aggregate numbers, not a surveillance file."

**1:20 — The close** *(hand back the table tent)*

> "Setup was five minutes — the QR code, this table tent, the iPad kiosk, all generated when I created the event. No new hardware, nothing to install. It's called ohm — *open house, measured*. The next listing you pitch, walk in with one of these reports and say: you'll get this after every open house. That wins listings."

> **ohm.sum28.com** — it's free. Set up your first open house right there in the meeting; it takes five minutes.
