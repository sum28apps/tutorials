## Voice rules (both products)

The products speak quietly; the marketing must too.

- **No exclamation marks. No ALL CAPS. No "revolutionary."** The proof carries the excitement.
- Product names are lowercase: **ohm**, **p2d** — even at the start of a sentence. Company name: **Sum28**.
- Numbers are the voice. "30 seconds", "five formats", "every line checked" — say the measurable thing, drop the adjective.
- Say **"presented with"**, never "sponsored by" or "ad", for ohm's partner placements.
- Never call visitors **"leads"** in anything a visitor or seller might see. They're visitors; the roster is the agent's.
- Don't overclaim the automated checks: p2d's Fair Housing screening is screening, not legal advice; final sign-off is the agent's. ohm's market comparison appears only when the data behind it is sufficient.
- Don't quote dollar prices in evergreen materials — link to the live pricing page instead. Prices in print go stale; links don't.
- UI words in demos and screenshots must match the product exactly: `+ new open house`, `end open house`, `send to seller`, `Read the photos`, `Write the copy`, `How we checked this`.

> **ohm** — open house, measured. Visitors sign in in 30 seconds, the home's packet reaches their phone instantly, and your seller gets a report worth reading.

> **p2d** — photo to description. Luxury listing copy written from your photos — with every line checked against them.

## Current facts to state plainly

- **ohm is free to use today** — no paid tiers. Say it; it closes.
- **p2d runs on credits** — don't quote dollar figures; link the pricing page.
