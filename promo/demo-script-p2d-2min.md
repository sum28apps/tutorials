# p2d — the 2-minute demo

*For a listing presentation, a brokerage lunch-and-learn, or a booth. One laptop, ~12 pre-picked photos of one listing, its MLS number.*

**Setup (before anyone's watching):** signed in; photo folder open; MLS number on a sticky note; one previously generated listing available in history (your safety net if the room's Wi-Fi dies).

---

**0:00 — The frame**

> "Agents on $5M+ listings either write the copy themselves at midnight or pay a copywriter a few hundred dollars a listing. The AI tools everyone's tried have a disqualifying habit: they invent. An 'unobstructed ocean view' the house doesn't have — at this price point, that sentence costs you the client. Two minutes — watch copy that *can't* do that."

**0:15 — Facts from the record** *(paste the MLS number, run the lookup)*

> "Beds, baths, square footage, year — even the sale history — straight from the MLS record. I typed nothing. Structural numbers now come from the record, never from a model's imagination."

**0:30 — The pedigree** *(type three letters in the Architect field)*

> "Watch — it's suggesting real architects, from years of actual luxury sales in this region. Get the name right and it's provenance; spell it wrong and it's a lawsuit from your own reputation. p2d treats names as precision cargo."

**0:45 — Read the photos** *(drop the photos, click `Read the photos`)*

> "Now it reads the photos — what's actually visible. Thirty seconds for a set like this."

*While it runs:* "The rule underneath is five words: **no claim without a pixel.** If the photos can't show it and the record doesn't say it, it cannot be written."

**1:05 — Confirm what we found** *(hover a feature; its photos light up)*

> "Everything it found — and *look* — hover a feature, and the photos that prove it light up. Nothing here was guessed. I can drop anything I don't want claimed. What survives this screen is the entire universe the copy may draw from. The agent stays in control."

**1:25 — Write the copy** *(click, wait, results appear)*

> "And… five formats. The long marketing piece. MLS remarks — already inside board limits, contact info already stripped. Headline, social caption, email. One PDF for the seller."

**1:40 — The receipt** *(open `How we checked this`)*

> "This is the part nothing else has. Every line, fact-checked by a *separate* model — the writer never grades its own homework. Anything that overreached was softened before I ever saw it. Fair Housing screened on every format. This is copy you publish without holding your breath — and this page is what you show the broker who asks 'but did AI write it?'"

**1:55 — The close**

> "Ten credits, about two minutes, five formats, every line checked. First listing takes ten minutes at **p2dapp.sum28.com** — bring your worst photo-only listing and see what it finds."

---

**If asked on the spot:**

- *"What happens to my photos?"* — "Processed in memory, not stored. It keeps the feature list it derived, not your images. And your inputs aren't training data."
- *"Who owns the copy?"* — "You do. Fully."
- *"My voice, though?"* — "A handful of questions — tone, opening, rhythm — five registers from Literary to Understated. Same facts, your telling. Happy to run the same house in two voices; that's the fun demo."
- *"Price?"* — "Plans and packs are on the pricing page — a finished listing is ten credits by default, small refinements one, hand edits free forever."
