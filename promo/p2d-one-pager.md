## English

### p2d — photo to description

**Luxury listing copy, written from your photos. Every line checked.**

At $5M+, listing copy has two jobs that fight each other: it must be beautiful, and it must be true. Generic AI does the first and quietly invents the second — the "unobstructed ocean view" the home doesn't have. A copywriter does both, for $150–500 a listing and days of turnaround.

p2d does both in about two minutes. Upload the photos you already have; point it at the MLS record. It reads what's actually visible, asks you to confirm what it found — every feature tied to the photos that prove it — and writes **five publish-ready formats**: marketing description, MLS remarks, headline, social caption, email.

**No claim without a pixel.**
If the photos can't show it and the record doesn't state it, p2d won't write it. An independent checker — never the writer grading itself — traces every sentence to its evidence. Overreach gets softened, automatically: "imported Carrara marble" becomes "stone countertops" unless the record says otherwise. Numbers come from the MLS record, checked digit-for-digit.

**Fair Housing, screened every time.**
Every format passes a compliance screen before you see it. The photo reader is structurally unable to describe people or neighborhoods — steering language can't get in, because it can't be expressed.

**Your voice, not a template's.**
A handful of plain questions — tone, opening, rhythm, length — and the copy reads like you wrote it on your best day. Five registers, from *Literary* to *Understated*. Same facts, your telling.

**Names treated as precision cargo.**
Architects and designers are matched against a registry built from years of real luxury sales. The one error this market never forgives — a misspelled pedigree — is the one p2d is built to prevent.

**The receipt.** Every generation ships with *How we checked this* — a claim-by-claim verification you can show a seller, a broker, or a skeptical buyer's agent.

## 中文

### p2d — photo to description（照片变文案）

**豪宅级房源文案，从你的照片写出。每一句都经过核查。**

在 $5M+ 价位，房源文案要同时做到两件互相打架的事：要美，也要真。通用 AI 擅长前者，却会悄悄虚构后者——写出这套房子并没有的"无遮挡海景"。人工文案师两者都行，代价是每单 $150–500 和几天的周转。

p2d 两分钟内两者都做到。上传你已有的照片，指向 MLS 记录。它读取照片里真实可见的内容，请你确认识别结果——每个特征都连着为它作证的照片——然后写出**五种可直接发布的格式**：营销长文、MLS remarks、标题、社媒短文、邮件。

**没有像素，不下断言。**
照片拍不出来、记录里没有的，p2d 不写。独立的校验模型——绝不让写手给自己打分——把每一句话追溯到证据。越界的说法被自动弱化："进口卡拉拉大理石"在记录佐证之前只会写成"石材台面"。数字一律来自 MLS 记录，逐位核对。

**Fair Housing，每次都筛。**
每种格式在你看到之前都过一遍合规筛查。读图模型在结构上就无法描述人和社区——steering 语言进不来，因为根本无法被表达。

**你的笔调，不是模板腔。**
几个平实的问题——语气、开场、节奏、长短——文案读起来就像你状态最好的那天亲笔写的。从"文学性"到"克制"五种语域可选。事实不变，讲法是你的。

**人名当精密件对待。**
建筑师与设计师对照多年真实豪宅成交数据建立的名录校验。这个市场唯一不原谅的错误——把门第拼错——恰是 p2d 被造出来防住的那个。

**核查回执。**每次生成都附带 *How we checked this*——逐句核查记录，可以拿给卖家、经纪公司、或挑剔的买方经纪看。
