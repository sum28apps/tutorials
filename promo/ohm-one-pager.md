## English

### ohm — open house, measured

**The paper sign-in sheet tells you nothing. This Saturday could.**

Every open house you host produces real evidence — who came, what they wanted, what happened next. On paper, that evidence dies in a binder. ohm turns it into three things: a welcome your visitors don't mind, a follow-up list you'll actually use, and a report your seller will actually read.

**For visitors — 30 seconds, then the home is in their hand.**
Tap the iPad or scan the code. Name, phone, email — done. The property packet (photos, description, 3D tour, your card) arrives on their phone before they reach the kitchen. In English, Spanish, Chinese, Korean, or Vietnamese.

**For you — five minutes of setup, then it runs itself.**
Create the open house; ohm generates the QR code, a print-ready table tent, and an iPad kiosk on the spot. Watch sign-ins live from your phone. The visitor list is yours — export it anytime. No hardware beyond what you own, nothing to install.

**For your seller — proof of work, every week.**
After each open house: visitors measured, packets delivered and opened, the trend against last weekend — plus your personal note. Aggregate numbers only, never visitor identities. When the market speaks, you'll have it in writing — for the price conversation, and for the next listing presentation.

**Quiet by design.**
Sign-in is never a condition of entry. Repeat visitors get a *welcome back*, not an error. Lost Wi-Fi queues and syncs silently. Text messages carry STOP and honor it automatically. The consent a visitor gives is snapshotted exactly as they saw it — cleaner evidence than any clipboard.

> ohm is free. Set up your first open house in five minutes at **ohm.sum28.com**.

### ohm — open house, measured（被测量的 open house）

**纸质签到表什么也告诉不了你。这个周六可以不一样。**

你办的每一场 open house 都在产生真实的证据——谁来了、想要什么、后来发生了什么。写在纸上，这些证据死在文件夹里。ohm 把它变成三样东西：访客不反感的迎接、你真正会用的跟进名单、卖家真正会读的报告。

**对访客 —— 30 秒，房子已经在手机里。**
点一下 iPad，或扫一下码。姓名、电话、邮箱——完成。资料包（照片、文案、3D 看房、你的名片）在访客走到厨房之前就到了手机上。支持英语、西班牙语、中文、韩语、越南语。

**对你 —— 建场五分钟，此后自动运转。**
创建 open house 的同时，二维码、可打印桌卡、iPad 签到台已经生成。手机上实时看签到。访客名单归你所有，随时导出。不用添置硬件，不用安装任何东西。
