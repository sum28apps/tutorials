# Lesson 5 · Credits, refines, and history

*The economics are simple: generation is the expensive thing; keeping it yours is cheap or free.*

## What you'll do

Understand the three price tags (10 / 1 / 0), when a refine beats a manual edit, and how history saves you next quarter.

## The three price tags

| Action | Default cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Generate a listing** | **10 credits** | The heavy lift: reading every distinct photo, drafting, independent line-by-line verification, compliance — all five formats |
| **AI refine** | **1 credit** — and the **first refine on each listing is free** | One focused pass, a tenth of the work |
| **Manual edit** | **Free, always** | Your words, your listing |

Defaults shown; plans, packs, and current pricing live at **`Buy credits`** → the pricing page — monthly plans with included credits, or one-time packs, checkout by Stripe.

The balance check happens **before** any expensive work starts — you'll never burn a generation to discover you were one credit short.

## Refine or edit by hand?

**Refine** when the instruction is about the *writing* — "quieter opening", "shorten the caption", "less about the kitchen". The result is re-checked: still grounded, still compliant. **Edit by hand** when you already know the exact words. Free, instant — and those words are yours rather than p2d's checked output.

A sensible rhythm: generate → one free refine to taste → hand-polish details. Total cost per listing: 10 credits.

## Your listings — the archive that compounds

Everything you generate is saved with its facts and its verification. This pays off on the timeline real listings actually live on:

- **The price adjustment.** Reopen the listing, refresh the price from the record, refine — no starting over.
- **Relisting in spring.** The confirmed features and your voice profile are waiting.
- **The proof page** stays available whenever a seller or broker asks how the copy was made.

## What p2d does *not* keep

Worth knowing as clearly as what it keeps: **your photos are processed in memory and not stored** — only the derived feature list is kept so refines stay grounded. Your uploads aren't training data, and the copy p2d writes for you is **yours** — p2d claims no ownership over your output.
