Lesson 1 · Create your first open house

Five minutes of setup, and ohm builds your sign-in kit for you.

What you'll do

Create an open house event, attach it to a listing, and walk away with a QR code, a printed table tent, and a kiosk link — all generated the moment the event exists.

The one idea to understand first

In ohm, materials belong to the listing; open houses belong to the listing too. A home usually hosts several open houses — back-to-back weekends are normal. Photos, the description, and the 3D-tour link are uploaded once, on the listing; every event (and every visitor packet) inherits them automatically. You never re-upload anything for round two.

Steps

1. Sign in and check your profile

ohm.sum28.com/app/login — one Sum28 account across ohm and p2d.

Visit settings before your first event. Your name, DRE number, and brokerage appear in the sign-in disclosure and on the seller report — legally meaningful text, so a typo here shows up everywhere.

2. + new open house

The form is deliberately short:

Field Notes
Address Or enter the MLS number and tap pull from mls — address, beds, baths, sqft, and list price fill themselves
Seller name + email The report's recipient. Editable until you send
Date, start, end Pick your window

Tap create open house. Anything you skipped — photos, description, links — can be added later without touching the event.

3. Collect the kit

Three things exist the instant the event does:

  • QR code — unique to this event. Every scan is automatically attributed, so your report can tell kiosk sign-ins from phone sign-ins.
  • Table tent PDF (download PDF) — a print-ready card in ohm's paper-and-ink style: address, large QR code. This is the zero-hardware mode: no iPad, no problem.
  • Kiosk link (open kiosk on this device) — turns any iPad's Safari into the entry sign-in station. Full screen, quiet, nothing to install.

4. Dry-run it once

Scan your own QR code with your phone. Sign yourself in. Watch the packet arrive by email and text. Ninety seconds now means zero surprises on Saturday.

Behind the scenes

  • The QR link and kiosk link use long unguessable tokens — nobody can stumble into your event by trying URLs.
  • Nothing about creating an event sends anything to your seller. Reports are generated after you close, and they never send themselves — you always review first.
