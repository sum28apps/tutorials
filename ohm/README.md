# ohm — open house, measured

ohm turns an open house from a stack of paper sign-in sheets into something you can measure, deliver, and show your seller.

**For visitors** — sign in in about 30 seconds on the entry iPad or their own phone, in English, Spanish, Chinese, Korean, or Vietnamese. The property packet (photos, description, 3D tour, your card) reaches their phone immediately.

**For you, the agent** — set up an open house in about five minutes. Watch sign-ins live from your phone. Your visitor list stays yours.

**For your seller** — a report after every open house: how many came, how the packet performed, what you did after. Every number traceable. No visitor names — aggregates only.

> ohm runs in the browser — the kiosk is Safari on your iPad, full screen, nothing to install. And ohm is free to use today.
